# Steam Workshop description

## English

**Recipe Guide** is a PC-only quality-of-life mod for Stacklands that makes the information panel easier to use and lets you explore discovered crafting recipes without losing your current context.

### Features

- Left-click cards, Ideas, quests, or Cardopedia entries to keep their information visible.
- A pin indicator shows when the original information panel is locked.
- Craftable names shown in the Ideas sidebar become clickable recipe links.
- Open recipes from the original information panel and continue opening ingredients one level at a time to the right.
- Up to 10 visible recipe levels, with repeated items in the current chain prevented.
- When the chain reaches the screen edge, older panels compress and stack so the newest recipe remains visible.
- Locked Ideas show an “not unlocked yet” message instead of revealing their recipe.
- Opening and closing uses Stacklands’ existing UI transitions.
- Supports the game’s localized recipe names and locked-recipe message.

### How to use

1. Open the Ideas sidebar or click a card in the game.
2. Left-click an entry to lock its information panel.
3. Move the cursor over an underlined craftable name and click it to open the next recipe level.
4. Click another entry, click empty map space, or press `Esc` to close the expanded recipe panels.

### Compatibility

- Stacklands on Steam for Windows PC.
- This mod does not add cards or change recipe costs.
- Recipe expansion is limited to craftable entries visible in the Ideas sidebar, preserving discovery progression.

## 中文

**Recipe Guide（配方指南）** 是一个仅支持 PC 的 Stacklands 便利性 Mod，用于锁定信息栏，并在不丢失当前查看内容的情况下逐层查看已经发现的制造配方。

### 功能

- 左键点击卡牌、主意、任务或 Cardopedia 条目，锁定当前信息。
- 原版信息栏右上角显示大头针，表示信息已锁定。
- 主意侧边栏中出现的可制造物品名称会变成可点击配方链接。
- 可以从原版信息栏打开第二层，并逐层向右查看原料配方。
- 最多支持 10 层配方信息，当前链条中已经出现过的物品不会重复展开。
- 信息栏触及屏幕右侧后，旧层级会压缩堆叠，保证最新配方仍然可见。
- 尚未解锁的主意只显示“尚未解锁”提示，不会提前显示配方内容。
- 展开和关闭使用 Stacklands 原有的界面动画。
- 支持游戏本地化名称和未解锁配方提示。

### 使用方法

1. 打开主意侧边栏，或在游戏中点击一张卡牌。
2. 左键点击条目，锁定信息栏。
3. 将鼠标移动到带下划线的可制造物品名称上并点击，打开下一层配方。
4. 点击其他条目、地图空白区域或按 `Esc`，关闭已经展开的配方层级。

### 兼容性

- 支持 Steam Windows PC 版 Stacklands。
- 本 Mod 不添加卡牌，也不修改配方数量或制造成本。
- 只有主意侧边栏中可见的可制造条目可以展开，以保持原版发现流程。
